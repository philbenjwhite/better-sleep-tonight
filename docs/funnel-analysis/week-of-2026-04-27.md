## Where the drop-off is happening

### Leak 1. Landing page: 257 → 83 (loss of 174 users, 68%)

The single biggest absolute loss. 174 visitors land on the page and never click "Begin." This is a **landing-page conversion problem**: hero copy, value prop clarity, and the perceived effort of "I have to watch videos to get a recommendation." It's not a quiz problem.

**Suggested action:** A/B test the headline, subheadline, and the "Begin" CTA. The site already supports running variants of the intro page, so we can ship tests without dev work on the funnel itself.

### Leak 2. Intro video: 83 → 39 (loss of 44 users, 53%)

Half of the people who click "Begin" never make it to the first question. They start watching the intro avatar video and quit before clicking through to the quiz.

**Likely culprits:**
- Intro video is too long, or too slow to get to the point
- Sound or autoplay issues on mobile making it feel broken
- The "Let's Go" button isn't visible or obviously clickable while the video plays

**Suggested action:** Shorten or re-cut the intro. Add tracking for "video started" vs "video finished" so we can tell whether people are bailing during the video or at the click step. A small session-replay sample of users who clicked Begin but didn't advance would also help.

### Leak 3. Mid-quiz: 39 → 4 (loss of 35 users, 90%)

Of the 39 who answered at least one question, only 4 made it to the recommendations and clicked "Book a Rest Test." Where exactly we lose them across the seven questions is **not visible in our current report**.

**Why we can't see it yet:** GA4 is collecting a single "quiz step" event with the question ID as a parameter. The standard events report rolls all of them into one row. The data is there, it just needs to be broken out in a custom GA4 exploration. Quick to do, no dev work needed.

**Also a measurement gap:** the recommendations screen itself isn't tracked unless the user clicks "Book a Rest Test." So users who see their recommendation and bounce are currently invisible. We should add a "recommendations viewed" event that fires whenever the screen is shown.

### Leak 4. End of funnel: 4 → 2 → 1

Numbers too small to draw conclusions. Two users clicked "Book a Rest Test" but didn't reach the final email-capture screen. They dropped during zipcode entry or in the store locator. One user reached that final screen and didn't submit an email.

These stages can wait until we have more volume.

## Caveats: read before quoting these numbers

### "Book Rest Test" is double or triple counted

Right now, every "Book a Rest Test" click fires the event **once per mattress shown** (2 or 3 times) instead of once per click. So:

- The "10" in the dashboard does not equal 10 clicks. It's actually **4 unique users** clicking the button, each click firing 2 or 3 times.
- The real ratio of "Book a Rest Test" clickers to "Quiz Complete" is **4 : 2**, not 10 : 2.

This needs a small dev fix before the dashboard number is trustworthy.

### "Quiz Complete" fires later than the name suggests

Today, "Quiz Complete" only fires when a user reaches the **final email-capture screen**. That's after recommendations, the post-recommendations video, zipcode entry, and the store locator. A user can see their mattress recommendation, click "Book a Rest Test," and never trigger Quiz Complete if they bail during zipcode entry.

**Decision to make:** should "completing the quiz" mean "saw their recommendation" (earlier) or "reached email capture" (current)? These tell very different stories about how the funnel is performing. Worth aligning with the team before locking dashboards.

### "Form Submit" is technically a Google Ads conversion event

The "Form Submit" column is wired to a Google Ads conversion tag rather than a standard GA4 form-submission event. It works correctly today, but if we add another Google Ads conversion later (e.g. for purchases), the same GTM trigger could end up double-counting. Worth a quick GTM audit before the next campaign.

### "Got past the intro" is a low bar

That 39-user count includes people who answered just one question and quit. It's the floor of engagement, not a measure of it. Once we have the per-question breakdown (Leak 3), this will become much more meaningful.

## Recommended next steps

In rough priority order:

1. **Pull the per-question breakdown in GA4** to find the real mid-quiz leak. No code changes, just a custom GA4 exploration.
2. **Fix the "Book a Rest Test" double-counting** so the dashboard number reflects actual clicks.
3. **Add a "recommendations viewed" event** so we can see how many users see their recommendation vs. how many click through.
4. **Align on what "Quiz Complete" should mean** ("saw recommendation" vs "reached email capture") and update the trigger accordingly.
5. **Add intro-video start/end tracking** so we can separate "bailed during video" from "watched but didn't click."
6. **Audit the GTM "Form Submit" trigger** to make sure it won't catch future Google Ads conversions by mistake.
7. **A/B test the landing page**, the biggest single leak in the funnel. Variant infrastructure is already in place.
